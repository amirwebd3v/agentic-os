# W2-11: Home: what needs you

**Points:** 4 · **Wave:** 5 · **Risk:** Med · **Depends on:** W2-08
**Read first:** contract §5 (`follow_up_on`), §8.2 (`PATCH`), `web/DESIGN.md`; plan §4 acceptance. Plan: [[implementation-plan]]. Skills by step: [[skills-map]].

## Summary
The page you open each day: what is done, what waits on you, and what comes next, for Career today and with Email and Social shown as "not connected".

## Steps
1. **Recall.** `/claude-mem:mem-search <ticket topic>`; before opening a long file, `/claude-mem:smart-explore` it. Keep claude-mem **off** in any session that opens a real CV, contact file, posting or the vault (see [[Workflow]]).
2. **Shape.** `/impeccable shape` the Home page: Operate mode; the task is "what do I do next?". Rows, not tiles (no stat-tile row, per `DESIGN.md`).
3. `PATCH /api/career/applications/:id` (status moves and `follow_up_on`, contract §8.2) with a `status` event per move; audit.
4. `HomeView.vue` at `/`: groups in this order: **Needs you** (flagged, draft ready, failed, render failed, pending reviews), **Follow-ups due** (date ≤ today), **In progress**, **Done** (finalised, applied, withdrawn). Each row shows company, role, status in words and the next step; "Mark applied" and "Set follow-up" inline. Email and Social: one quiet row each, "not connected".
5. `/impeccable onboard` for the first-run empty state (no master yet: "import your master CV", with the path to put the files); `/impeccable clarify` for status words and button labels; `/impeccable harden` for long titles, German text and 100+ rows.
6. Look at it with `/run` and `built-in-browser`, light and dark, 1280 and 800 px; then `/impeccable audit` and `detect`.
7. **Check.** `pnpm verify` (judge by exit code) → `/simplify` → `/ponytail:ponytail-review` on the diff → `/code-review medium`.
8. **Close.** Tick the acceptance list; `save_memory` (cause → fix for any mistake) and a `CLAUDE.md` Lesson in the same PR if one happened; `/claude-mem:babysit` the PR; `/claude-mem:handoff` if you pause. After reporting ready, push nothing (rule 8).
