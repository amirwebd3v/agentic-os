# W2-07: Tailor pipeline

**Points:** 5 · **Wave:** 3 · **Risk:** High · **Depends on:** W2-03, W2-04, W2-05, W2-06
**Read first:** contract §3, §4 (steps 2–4), §4.2, §4.3, §5, §6, §10 (W2-07); plan §3, §7 R1–R3. Plan: [[implementation-plan]]. Skills by step: [[skills-map]].

## Summary
From a posting and your master CV, produce a CV and a cover letter in which every line cites a bullet of yours, and a plain-code verifier that rejects anything it cannot trace.

## Steps
1. **Recall.** `/claude-mem:mem-search <ticket topic>`; before opening a long file, `/claude-mem:smart-explore` it. Keep claude-mem **off** in any session that opens a real CV, contact file, posting or the vault (see [[Workflow]]).
2. **Prompt shape** (`/claude-api`): the redacted master CV goes in the stable, cached system block; the requirements and posting facts go in the uncached context (R3). Models and effort per step: match `work`/`medium`, draft `work`/`high` (R2). No sampling parameters, no forced `tool_choice`.
3. `jobs/career-match.ts`: `Selection` (§4.2); deterministic checks (ids exist, bullets belong to their entry); `coverage` computed by code; nothing selected → `fail_code: 'no_match'`.
4. `jobs/career-draft.ts`: `Draft` (§4.3), in the **master's language** (R11); contact only as placeholders; employer, title and dates are not model output (render copies them by `entry_id`). Attempt 2 receives the violation list.
5. `server/src/career/verify.ts` + `jobs/career-verify.ts`: every rule of contract §6, one fixture each; the report is stored, never thrown; attempt 1 fails → draft attempt 2; attempt 2 fails → `status: 'flagged'`; never a third.
6. Migration `0006_career.sql` (committed by the coordinator before Wave 2): statuses, steps, events (ids, counts and codes only, no text). Status moves are transactional with the event row.
7. Fixtures: a synthetic master and contact file, postings from W2-05, a fake `llm` that returns good, bad and adversarial drafts.
8. **Check.** `pnpm verify` (judge by exit code) → `/simplify` → `/ponytail:ponytail-review` on the diff → `/code-review medium`, **`/security-review`** (prompts, privacy) and, at the end, **`/claude-api prompt-audit`** on the two prompts.
9. **Close.** Tick the acceptance list; `save_memory` (cause → fix for any mistake) and a `CLAUDE.md` Lesson in the same PR if one happened; `/claude-mem:babysit` the PR; `/claude-mem:handoff` if you pause. After reporting ready, push nothing (rule 8).
